# Commercial Licensing for fSnippetCli

Most people never need this page. fSnippetCli's source code is Apache-2.0, and official
builds are free for individuals, education, non-profits, open-source projects and
organizations up to 250 concurrent copies.

## When you need a commercial license

| Situation                                                                                               | Why                      |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------- |
| More than 250 concurrent installs of **official builds** within one organization (including affiliates) | DISTRIBUTION-TERMS.md §3 |
| Reselling official builds, or bundling them in a paid product or service                                | DISTRIBUTION-TERMS.md §3 |
| Offering fSnippetCli to others as a hosted / managed service                                            | DISTRIBUTION-TERMS.md §3 |
| Using the fSnippetCli name or logo on your own product or service                                       | TRADEMARK.md             |

Building from source and using your own builds is **never** subject to these terms.

## What you get

* A perpetual license for the agreed number of copies (or attribution-free output),
  covering all versions released during the support period.
* Priority handling of bug reports.
* An invoice your procurement department will accept.

## Pricing

Pricing is per organization and scales with the number of copies. Send us the
approximate number of installs (or the intended use) and we will reply with a quote.
